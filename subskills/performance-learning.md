# Subskill: Campaign Performance Learning

Editorial scores do not prove campaign value.

---

## 1. Record Per Clip

- Campaign ID, approved views, qualified views.
- Viewed vs swiped-away, average view duration, completion rate.
- Shares/comments per 1k views.
- Human approval/rejection reason.
- Treatment, format, hook, caption style, thumbnail variant.

## 2. Optimize

Maximize qualified approved views per unit of production cost, not the number of PASS verdicts.

## 3. Do Not

- Train on raw view count alone.
- Treat a high editorial score as performance proof.
- Hide rejected or failed clips from the learning set.
