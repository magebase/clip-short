# clip-short v2.4 — Fail-Closed Semantic Quality Contract

## 1. Result model

- `PASS`: every required check ran and returned positive evidence.
- `FAIL`: a check ran and found a defect.
- `NOT_CHECKED`: a required check did not run; blocks PASS.
- `HUMAN_REVIEW`: ambiguity or missing capability.

No editorial score overrides a failed semantic, object, speaker, diagram, thumbnail, campaign, or technical check.

## 2. Semantic reviewer

Sole permitted model: `muse-spark-1.3-contributor` via `Meta Model API`, Responses API, requested effort `max`.

Required: `provider`, `method`, `model`, `reviewer_version`, `reviewed_output_sha256`, `inspected_frames`, `explanation`, `findings`.
No other model, heuristic, ensemble, or fallback may issue a semantic PASS. A model/effort mismatch is `CRITIC_MODEL_POLICY_VIOLATION`.

## 3. Narration-to-visual timeline

Every beat records `start`, `end`, `narration`, `expected_objects`, `required_visual`, `caption_safe_zone`, `evidence_frame_sha256`.
Objects cannot be credited before their narration timestamp.

## 4. Shot-level speaker QA

Every camera cut records `start`, `end`, `active_speaker`, `visible_person`, `shot_type`, `intentional_cutaway`, `decision`.
Unjustified `MIC_ONLY` while speech is active is critical.

## 5. Diagram state validation

Every required element records `name` and `bbox_norm`. Checks: `all_elements_visible`, `all_labels_readable`, `no_edge_clipping`, `semantic_state_distinct`, `caption_collision`.
A 2% safe margin is mandatory.

## 6. Object identity and prominence

Identity requires `vision_model` or validated `detector_class`; `dark_blobs`, `text_labels`, and `large_shape` are pre-filters only.
Prominence is normalized 0..1. Object and caption bboxes must not collide or touch safe edges.

## 7. Thumbnail

Actual OCR and semantic match are required booleans. `not_checked` blocks PASS. One headline, face/eye protection, crop completeness, mobile readability.

## 8. Provenance

Per output: `git_sha`, `git_clean`, `renderer_source_hash`, `dependency_lock_hash`, `build_config_hash`, `output_sha256`, `reviewer_version` equal to artifact version.

## 9. Performance

Record qualified campaign views, AVD, completion, shares/comments, approval decisions, and treatment variants. Optimize qualified views per production cost, not PASS count.
